# Business Model & Impact

## 1. The Value Proposition
Information is potential profit, but **synthesized information** is realized profit. We sell "Clarity" and "Speed".

## 2. Target Audience
*   **Retail Traders**: Individual investors who want institutional-grade tools without the Bloomberg Terminal price tag ($24k/year).
*   **Hedge Funds / Prop Shops**: Quantitative firms looking for alternative data signals (Data Feed API).
*   **Commodity Corporates**: Mining companies or manufacturers needing to monitor supply chain sentiment.

## 3. Revenue Streams
1.  **SaaS Subscription (B2C)**
    *   **Free Tier**: Delayed data, top 3 narratives only.
    *   **Pro Tier ($49/mo)**: Real-time processing, unlimited narratives, deep-dive analytics.
2.  **API Licensing (B2B)**
    *   Selling the raw "Narrative Sentiment" signals to algo-trading funds to incorporate into their automated strategies.

## 4. Market Impact
*   **Democratization**: Leveling the playing field between Wall Street (who have armies of analysts) and the independent trader.
*   **Efficiency**: By identifying valid narratives faster, markets can price in information more efficiently, reducing random volatility.

## 5. Scalability
*   **Asset Expansion**: The same engine powering Silver/Gold monitoring can easily be retrained for Oil, Wheat, Bitcoin, or even single stocks (e.g., TSLA).
*   **Language Expansion**: AI models can cross-lingual transfer, allowing us to ingest news in Chinese or Spanish and present it in English.
