# Future Roadmap

## Phase 1: Deepening Intelligence (Next 3 Months)
*   **Source Expansion**: Integrate Twitter (X) firehose and Reddit (r/WallStreetBets, r/Commodities) to capture retail sentiment vs. institutional news.
*   **Temporal Analysis**: Add "Timeline View" to see how a narrative started, peaked, and died.
*   **User Accounts**: Allow users to save/bookmark specific narratives.

## Phase 2: Actionable Trading (3-6 Months)
*   **Alert System**: Push notifications: "New 'Supply Shock' Narrative detected in Copper."
*   **Backtesting Engine**: Allow users to run the AI on historical data. "If I had traded on Echelon signals in 2023, how much would I have made?"
*   **Portfolio Integration**: Connect to user's brokerage account to suggest hedging strategies based on narrative exposure.

## Phase 3: Platform Expansion (6+ Months)
*   **Multi-Asset Support**: Expand beyond Silver/Gold to Oil, Natural Gas, Wheat, and Crypto.
*   **LLM "Chat with Market"**: A chatbot interface (RAG) where users can ask: "Summarize the bearish arguments for Silver right now."
*   **Mobile App**: Native iOS/Android application.
